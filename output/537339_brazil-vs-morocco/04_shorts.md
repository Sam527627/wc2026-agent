# 3 Shorts

## Short 1
**Hook:** Brazil stunned by Morocco!

Brazil's World Cup dreams were put to the test last night, and the result will send shockwaves through the entire tournament. A 1-1 draw against Morocco has thrown Group C into chaos - but what does it really mean for the favorites?

## Short 2
**Hook:** Morocco's tactics exposed Brazil!

Morocco's coach set up his team to absorb pressure and hit Brazil on the counter, using their pacey wingers to exploit the spaces left by Brazil's attacking full-backs. As the game wore on, Brazil's possession dominance began to wane, and Morocco started to gain a foothold in the match.

## Short 3
**Hook:** Brazil's struggles continue!

Brazil's possession dominance was not enough to secure a win against Morocco. The Seleção came into this match as overwhelming favorites, but Morocco's clever tactics and Brazil's own struggles in front of goal have raised more questions than answers.
